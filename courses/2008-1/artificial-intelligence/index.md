## {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-bullseye" %}Course Objectives

- To understand the principles of cognitive information processing in the human brain
- To get ideas on developing computational methods to imitate human cognition, learning, and memory
- To acquire the theoretical and technical tools for application of artificial intelligence
- To understand the history and future prospects of bio-inspired artificial intelligence
