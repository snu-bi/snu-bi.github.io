The mLife project investigated how to automatically **identify and model human behaviors** from mobile life-log data collected via smartphone sensors, enabling intelligent personalized mobile applications.

## Research Focus

As smartphones became ubiquitous, they opened a window into daily human life through embedded sensors: GPS, accelerometer, gyroscope, microphone, camera. mLife asked: how can we mine these sensor streams to understand who you are and what you do?

## Key Contributions

- **Context-aware activity recognition**: inferring not just *what* users do but *why* and *where*
- **Multimodal mobile sensing**: fusing heterogeneous sensor modalities (location, motion, audio)
- Machine learning-based **behavioral pattern discovery** for personalized recommendations
- Scalable mining of continuous behavioral patterns from real-world daily logs
