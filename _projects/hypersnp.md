HyperSNP applied **hypergraph-based computational models** to the analysis of large-scale Single Nucleotide Polymorphism (SNP) data — exploring how hypergraph representations can better capture genetic relationships.

## Background

SNPs are the most common type of genetic variation. Understanding how combinations of SNPs relate to traits and diseases requires modeling interactions among many variants simultaneously — far beyond what pairwise graphs can represent.

## Technical Approach

- **Hypergraph representations**: edges connecting more than two SNP nodes simultaneously, capturing multi-locus interactions
- Algorithms for discovering significant multi-SNP modules from large-scale genotyping data
- Machine learning-based scoring of hyperedge significance

## Connection to Lab Research

HyperSNP is part of the lab's broader program of applying hypernetwork/hypergraph architectures — developed for language, vision, and cognitive tasks — to genomics and bioinformatics, demonstrating their universality across domains.
