DeepClone aimed to create **deep-learning-based digital clones of humans** by capturing behavioral patterns through wearable sensors and long-term lifelogging, enabling a personal service robot to anticipate and schedule tasks on the user's behalf.

## Vision

What if your robot knew your schedule better than you do? DeepClone sought to build a digital twin of the user's daily behavioral patterns — their routines, preferences, and habits — so a service robot could autonomously plan and execute tasks without being told.

## Sensing Modalities

- Smart glasses (first-person visual context)
- Smartwatches (motion, biometrics)
- Brain scanners (cognitive state)
- Body sensors (physiological signals)

## Technical Approach

- Long-term real-world lifelogging as training data
- Reverse-engineering individual routines and preferences from multimodal sensor streams
- Deep learning models for behavioral pattern learning and future task prediction
- Closely related to the IITP-funded **StarLab** project on Cognitive Agents
