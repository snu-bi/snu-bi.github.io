The BrainNet project investigated **higher-order relational structure in cortical brain networks** by identifying hyperedges — groupings of multiple brain regions that form functionally meaningful modules beyond pairwise connections.

## Motivation

Standard brain connectivity analyses use pairwise edges (region A connects to region B). But cognition emerges from coordinated activity across many regions simultaneously. Hyperedges capture these higher-order functional groupings that pairwise graphs miss.

## Methods

- **Hypernetwork and hypergraph models** applied to structural MRI data (cortical thickness)
- **Evolutionary algorithms** and information-theoretic methods to discover significant multi-regional modules
- Analysis of brain network topology for cognitive trait prediction

## Application

- **IQ estimation** from brain network topology: predicting cognitive performance from structural connectivity patterns
- Neurodevelopmental and neurological disorder analysis

## Data

Cortical thickness measurements from structural MRI, representing cross-region morphological relationships in the human cerebral cortex.
