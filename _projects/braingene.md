The BrainGene project developed a **DNA chip-based gene expression analysis system** covering approximately 10,000 rat genes (~30,000 total), targeting the identification of genes specifically expressed during vertebrate brain development.

## Approach

- **DNA microarray** experiments measuring expression of ~10,000 rat genes across developmental stages
- **Unsupervised machine learning** for discovering stage-specific and region-specific expression clusters
- **Bayesian network-based dependency analysis** to model regulatory relationships between genes
- **In situ hybridization profiling** for spatial expression pattern validation

## Web Database

A gene expression database with a web interface was constructed to make results accessible to the broader neuroscience community.

## Collaboration

Conducted jointly with the Schools of Medicine at **Hanyang University** and **Korea University** — an interdisciplinary project bridging computational machine learning and experimental neurobiology.
