DeepAction applied **deep learning** techniques to recognize and analyze the activities of television viewers, modeling viewer behaviors and interactions during TV watching.

## Context

Understanding what TV viewers are actually doing — cooking, relaxing, exercising, working — while watching television enables smarter content recommendations and interactive TV experiences. DeepAction built on the lab's expertise in activity recognition, video understanding, and lifelong learning from multimodal sensory data.

## Technical Approach

- Convolutional and recurrent neural network architectures for temporal activity modeling
- Multimodal fusion of visual and contextual signals
- Built upon earlier lab work in mLife (mobile behaviors) and Videome (video understanding)
