RoboMotion investigated **machine learning approaches for generating flexible robot arm motions** by learning from observed human activity sequences — enabling robots to watch and imitate humans.

## Collaboration

Conducted in collaboration with the **IAS Group at Technische Universität München (TUM)**, Germany, combining the SNU lab's machine learning expertise with TUM's robotics engineering capabilities.

## Technical Approach

- **Spatio-temporal feature learning** from human activity video
- **Semantic rule extraction**: translating observed activities into transferable symbolic rules
- Transfer of human motion patterns to robotic systems via learned policies

## Key Publication

- Ramirez-Amaro, Kim, Zhang, Beetz, Cheng (2013). "Enhancing Human Action Recognition Through Spatio-Temporal Features and Semantic Rules." *IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots.*
