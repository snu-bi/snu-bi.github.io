The Video Turing Test (VTT) project aimed to develop AI agents capable of **human-level comprehension of video narratives** and open-ended dialogue about video content, using TV drama and movie data as a testbed for measuring machine intelligence.

## Key Contributions

- **VTT Framework**: A blind evaluation methodology to assess AI human-likeness in video understanding
- **DramaQA**: A large-scale character-centered video QA benchmark based on hierarchical story structures
- Multimodal (vision, language, audio) representation learning
- Hierarchical question answering and character grounding/re-identification
- Scene graph-based causal reasoning over long-form video

## Benchmark: DramaQA

DramaQA is built from Korean TV drama episodes and provides:
- Multi-level difficulty QA (4 levels based on memory and reasoning complexity)
- Character-centered annotation with visual grounding
- Rich multimodal context (video clips, subtitles, scene descriptions)
