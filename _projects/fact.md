The FACT project investigated **machine learning methods for automatically filtering and classifying text** from large-scale document collections — foundational work in what we now call information retrieval and NLP.

## Approach

- **Boosting-based methods** using Naïve Bayes classifiers as weak learners
- Leveraging term frequency information with probabilistically accurate confidence ratios
- Improved performance on **TREC filtering track** benchmarks (a major international competition)

## SCAIR: Lab's Custom Filtering Engine

The lab developed **SCAIR** (SNU Cognitive AI Research), its own text filtering engine, used in TREC-8 experiments covering:
- Ad hoc retrieval
- Adaptive filtering
- Web search
- Question answering tracks

## Historical Context

FACT was conducted at a time when the web was exploding with text content and automated filtering was a pressing need. This work laid the groundwork for later research in NLP, information extraction, and eventually the lab's multimodal learning projects.
