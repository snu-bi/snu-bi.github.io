Videome investigated how machines can learn **cognitive representations directly from streams of digital video**, drawing inspiration from how the human brain constructs episodic memory and conceptual knowledge.

## Approach

Rather than training on curated image datasets, Videome exposed the model to continuous video streams — just as a child watches TV — and learned structured knowledge incrementally.

## Key Technical Contributions

- **Multimodal Hypernetwork** architectures for evolutionary concept learning from cartoon video streams
- **Deep Concept Hierarchy (DCH)**: a model enabling progressive, lifelong abstraction of visual-linguistic knowledge
- Demonstrated on the children's series *Pororo* as a large-scale cartoon video corpus

## Results

- Presented at **AAAI 2015**: incremental concept learning from video yields structured vision-language knowledge
- Applicable to scene recognition, dialogue understanding, and visual question answering
