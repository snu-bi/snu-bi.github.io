Lavatar was a research project focused on building **autonomous learning agents** that operate as avatars inside virtual world environments, investigating how machine learning could enable avatars to acquire and adapt behaviors through interaction.

## Context

As virtual worlds (Second Life, online game environments) grew in the late 2000s, Lavatar explored them as training grounds for autonomous agents — rich interactive environments where learning algorithms could be tested in social, physical, and goal-directed scenarios.

## Technical Approach

- Bayesian and hypernetwork models for in-world behavior learning
- Reinforcement learning for goal-directed avatar behavior
- Social interaction modeling with other agents and users in shared virtual spaces
