The E-Learn project investigated using **Bayesian networks** to build probabilistic learner models that represent and reason about individual student knowledge states and learning styles.

## Core Technology

- **Probabilistic learner modeling**: representing what a student knows and how confidently
- **Bayesian inference**: updating the learner model from interaction data in real time
- **Adaptive content delivery**: selecting instructional content that most efficiently fills knowledge gaps

## Connection to Lab Research

Extends the lab's core expertise in hierarchical Bayesian networks and probabilistic graphical models — developed for biological data analysis — to the domain of intelligent tutoring and adaptive e-learning.

## Impact

Anticipates the modern wave of AI in education (AI tutors, adaptive learning platforms, personalized curricula) by grounding educational adaptation in principled probabilistic reasoning.
