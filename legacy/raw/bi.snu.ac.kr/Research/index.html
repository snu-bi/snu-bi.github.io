Research in SNU Biointelligence Lab

Our research focuses on bio-inspired and bio-based intelligence technologies, i.e. biointelligence. Current work includes computational modeling and biomolecular implementation of biological information processing at the molecular, evolutionary, and neural levels and their application to building learning and inference machines for solving problems in molecular biology and life sciences.

Learning and Evolution

  • Bayesian Evolutionary Computation
  • Probabilistic Graphical Models

Biological Discovery

  • DNA Chip Data Mining
  • Gene Finding
  • Protein Classification

Molecular Inference

  • Molecular Evolutionary Computing (MEC)
  • Bioinference Chips

Current Projects

  • DMDM: DNA microarray data mining using unsupervised learning techniques
  • LEONN: Learning and evolution of neural networks
  • MEC: Molecular evolutionary computing
  • MicroGene: Microbial gene identification using probabilistic graphical models
  • MrHumor: A personalized Internet agent that recommends humors and jokes
  • ProClass: Protein classification based on molecular sequence and text information

Completed Projects

  • AGIPT: Adaptive genetic programming systems for flexible information processing
  • ALENN: Active learning evolutionary neural networks
  • BioText: Information extraction from biological texts based on Markov models
  • eClerk: A learning agent for recommending products at a virtual shopping mall
  • ECTOP: Evolutionary computing theory for optimization
  • EOS: Online learning of robot behaviors with evolvable hardware
  • FACT: Text filtering and classification from large-scale document collections
  • GAMAR: Genetic algorithms for multicast routing
  • MACS: Evolving homing and herding behaviors of multiple robotic agents
  • NACST: Nucleic acid computing simulation toolbox
  • NetMining: Network intrusion detection using active datamining techniques
  • ScaiTrec: SCAI at Text REtrieval Conference (TREC)
  • SIFOGA: Statistical inference as a foundation of genetic algorithms
  • STAR: Statistical learning for ambiguity resolution
  • WAIR: Intelligent learning agents for personalized information retrieval on the Web
