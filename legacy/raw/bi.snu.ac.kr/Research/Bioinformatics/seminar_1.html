Bioinformatics Seminar 2001

(2001. Feb - Mar)

This Seminar of Bioinformatics Research Group at SCAI is focused on developing and applying machine learning algorithms for the analysis of genomic and proteomic data with diverse methods. Such tools currently specialized in gene expression data.
