Course 419.206: Data Structures

Department of Computer Engineering, Seoul National University

Instructor: Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang
Classroom: 301-203
Schedule: Mon 9-10am, Wed 9-10am, Fri 9-10am
Textbook: E. Horowitz, S. Sahni, and D. Mehta, Fundamentals of Data Structures in C++, Freeman and Company, 1995.

Course Outline

  • Week 1: Introduction
  • Week 2: Analysis of Algorithms

  • Week 4: Sparse Matrices, Strings
  • Week 5: Stacks and Queues
  • Week 6: Evaluation of Expressions
  • Week 7: Multiple Stacks and Queues
  • Week 8: Midterm Exam
  • Week 9: Singly Linked Lists, Circular Lists
  • Week 10: Doubly Linked Lists, Generalized Lists
  • Week 11: Binary Trees, Tree Traversal
  • Week 12: Threaded Binary Trees, Heaps
  • Week 13: Selection Trees, Forests
  • Week 14: Graphs, Minimum Spanning Trees
  • Week 15: Shortest Paths, Activity Networks
  • Week 16: Final Exam
