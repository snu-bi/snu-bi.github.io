Course 2071.402:
Brain and Computation (2009)
 
  • School of Computer Science and Engineering, Seoul National University
  • Instructor: Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang
  • TA: Hoon-Hee Kim (Office : 138-409, Tel : 02-880-5890)
  • Classroom: 83-501
  • Time: Thursday, 14:30~17:30
  • Objectives:
    • To understand the principles of information coding and processing in the brain.
    • To learn mathematical and computational tools for modeling brain function at the neuron and network levels.
    • To build computational models of learning and memory in the brain networks.
  • Textbook:
    • Fundamentals of Computational Neuroscience, Thomas Trappenberg , 2002.
  • References:
    • Principles of Neural Science, Eric R. Kandel, J.H. Schwartz, Thomas M. Jessell, 2000
    • Nature Neuroscience 2000 supplement - Computational Approaches to Brain Function, BI Seminar Link
  • Evaluation:
    • Mid-Term exams (45%)
    • Final exam  (45%)
    • Presence and participation in discussion (10%)
