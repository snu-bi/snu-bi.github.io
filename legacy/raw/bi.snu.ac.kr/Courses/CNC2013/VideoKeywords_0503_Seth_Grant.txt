The problem of Synaptic Complexity
Seth Grant (2010.09.28)

Shortcomings of the anatomical model
 - regionalisation of behaviors is not clear cut
 - does not fit well with evolutionary and comparative species data
 - does not take into account genomic or synapse evolution

High complexity in synapse proteome

Modular protain interation networks
 - Upstream & downstream modules

Synaptic origins & evolution
 - Synapse proteins in organisms lacking neurons

Evolution of Combinations/Organization of complexes

Overlapping combinations of postsynaptic subnetworks modulate each trait and are used in specific environments

Genetic architecture of innate behaviours (Diagram at 18:00)

Implications
- subtle changes in gene expression or protein in a species could give it an advantage in a particular environmental niche
- may explain, in part, the larger behavioural repertoire and flexibility of vertebrates
- common underlying molecular mechanism in postsynaptic complexes for innate and learned responses
- disease mutations shift repertoire of innate (and learned) responses in particular ways, which characterises the disease.
