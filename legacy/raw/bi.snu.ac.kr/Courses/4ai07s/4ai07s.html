Course 4190.408:
Artificial Intelligence: Biointelligence (2007)
3391.506: Computational Neuroscience
132.651: Connectionist Modeling of Cognitive Processes
  • School of Computer Science and Engineering, Seoul National University
  • Instructor: Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang
  • TA: Chan-hoon Park (Room : 301-417, Tel : 880-1835)
  • Classroom: 302-106
  • Time: Tue 13:00-14:15 and Thu 13:00-14:15
  • Objectives
      - To understand the principles of cognitive information processing in the human brain
      - To get ideas on developing computational methods to imitate the human cognition, learning, and memory
      - To acquire the theoretical and technical tools for application of artificial intelligence
      - To understand the history and future prospects of bio-inspired artificial intelligence
  • Textbook
    • Cognitive Neuroscience: The Biology of the Mind, M.S. Gazzaniga, R.B. Ivry, and G.R. Mangun, Norton & Company, 2002. [CNS]
    • Artificial Intelligence: A New Synthesis, Nils J. Nilsson, Morgan Kaufmann, 1998. [AI]
  • References
    1. Molecular Nanobiointelligence Computers: Computer Science Meets Biotechnology, Nonotechnology, and Cognitive Science, Communications of the Korea Information Science Society, 23(5):41-56, 2005. [NBIC, 2005]
    2. Hyperinteractionism: Investigations into Artificial Minds Beyond Symbolism, Connectionism, and Dynamicism Natural Science, 144-165, Summer 2005. [HI, 2005]
    3. Artificial Intelligence: Past, Present and Future (in Korean), In Computers in the 21st Century, Chap. 11, Kyohaksa, pp.176-200, 1998. [AI-PPF, 1998]
