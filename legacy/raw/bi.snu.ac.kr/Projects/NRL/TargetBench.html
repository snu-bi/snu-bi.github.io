TargetBench Research Team

Our research focuses on making DNA Chip contents for a specific application, i.e. the target gene selection. The work includes the target selection technique from clinical databases as well as public databases such as OMIM and the filtering technique from the expression database Ultimately, the main goal is to development algorithms and tools to select the most suitable gene contents corresponding to a specific purpose. The target gene selection algorithms require to study the gene regulatory pattern analysis and the prediction for transcription factor binding site.
