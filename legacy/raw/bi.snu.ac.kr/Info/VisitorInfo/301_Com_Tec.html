From Seoul Nat'al Univ. Station To Engineering  Building 301 ("ShinKongHakGwan")

By Bus
  1. Get out of the Seoul Nat'l Univ. station through Exit 3
  2. Take the bus No. 413 (600 Won, takes 10 min.)
  3. Get off the bus in front of the New Engeering Building
  (last stop, 15-story building with blue glass windows)
  4. Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang's office: 301-401 (Tel: 880-1833)
      - Biointelligence Lab: 301-417 (Tel: 880-1835), 301-451 (Tel: 880-7302),                                              301-419  (Tel: 880-1847)
      - Research Institute of Advanced Computer Technology:
          138-409 (Tel: 880-5890)
 

By Taxi
  1. Get out of the Seoul Nat'l univ. station through the Exit 3
  2. Take a taxi (about 2,000 Won, takes 7 minutes)
  3. Get off the taxi in front of the New Engeering Building
  4. Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang's office: 301-401 (Tel: 880-1833)
      - Biointelligence Lab: 301-417 (Tel: 880-1835), 301-451 (Tel: 880-7302),                                              301-419  (Tel: 880-1847)
      - Research Institute of Advanced Computer Technology:
          138-409 (Tel: 880-5890)
