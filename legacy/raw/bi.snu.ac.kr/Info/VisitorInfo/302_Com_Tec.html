From Seoul Nat'al Univ. Station To New Engineering  Building 302

By Bus
  1. Get out of the Seoul Nat'l Univ. station through Exit 3
  2. Take the bus No. 5511, 5513 (900 Won, takes 10 min.)
  3. Get off the bus in front of the New Engeering Building
  (last stop, 10-story building with blue glass windows)
  4. Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang's office: 302-323 (Tel: 880-1833)
      - Biointelligence Lab: #314-1, Bldg.302 (Tel: 880-1847, 1835)
      - Research Institute of Advanced Computer Technology:
          138-409 (Tel: 880-5890)
 

By Taxi
  1. Get out of the Seoul Nat'l univ. station through the Exit 3
  2. Take a taxi (about 4,000 Won, takes 7 minutes)
  3. Get off the taxi in front of the New Engeering Building
  4. Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang's office: 302-323 (Tel: 880-1833)
      - Biointelligence Lab: : #314-1, Bldg.302 (Tel: 880-1847, 1835)
      - Research Institute of Advanced Computer Technology:
          : 138-409 (Tel: 880-5890, 9131)

  • Approximate time of travel from Incheon International Airport to Seoul Nat'l Univ. station: 90 minutes
  • Approximate time of travel from Downtown (City Hall) to Seoul Nat'l Univ. station: 35 minutes
