Getting to Seoul Nat'l Unv. Station ("SeoulDae-lpKu Yeok")

1. From Incheon International Airport to SNU station


 

Unfortunately, subway connections are unavailable from Incheon Int'al airport to Seoul. Instead, passengers can take a bus or airport limousine to get to their hotel or to SNU. To get to Seoul National University station, take airport bus number 600, 606 (get off at Express Bus Terminal), or 609 (Kangnam Station). Finding a subway should be easy from there. From Kangnam station, simply take a subway to Kyodae/Sadang, and get off at Seoul Nat'l Univ. station. From Express Bus Terminal, take a Subway to Kyodae, and then transfer to line no.2 to Sadang. See Airport Bus Schedule. Note that Kyodae is also called "Seoul National Univ. of Education" - it is easily confused with Seoul National University station which is only on no. 2 LINE.

2. From Downtown Seoul (City Hall) to Seoul National University station
Take subway number 4 LINE to the SaDang Station, transfer to the   number 2 LINE and get off at Seoul Nat'l Univ. Station. Average traveling time to SNU is 30 to 40 minutes.

3. From Kangnam/Jamsil area to Seoul National University station

Take subway number 2 LINE to Kyodae/Shindorim. Get off at Seoul Nat'l Univ.station.
